UKRAINE  ·  LEAD STORY  ·  KYIV AND POLAND
RUSSIA POUNDS UKRAINE — MISSILES LAND IN POLAND
400+ drones and 110 missiles in one night; NATO jets scrambled as debris falls on Polish soil
Russia’s overnight barrage kills at least 8 people in Kyiv, injures 28 and damages three bridges; a ballistic strike on Kryvyi Rih on Saturday kills 2 more. Two Russian missiles and a suspected jet-powered drone come down in Poland — at Nowy Lipowiec, Rudnik nad Sanem and Molodycz. Tusk calls it a “deliberate provocation”; Polish and NATO jets scramble, Patriots activate, and France calls a UN Security Council meeting.
CLIMATE  ·  COP31  ·  ANTALYA
COP31 RUNS INTO OVERTIME
Talks in Antalya run past Friday’s deadline into Saturday as the venue is boxed up around negotiators. The draft final text mentions no fossil fuels; a third text is promised Saturday morning. Nearly 200 nations are involved; next summit: Addis Ababa, November 2027.
MIDDLE EAST  ·  GULF
IRAN SAYS IT DOWNED A U.S. DRONE
Iran’s Revolutionary Guards say a new air-defence system shot down a US MQ-9 over the Strait of Hormuz on Saturday — the 58th such claim — then a US MQ-1 over Qeshm Island. The US military has no comment; tankers were hit by projectiles near Khasab, Oman.
MARKETS  ·  FRIDAY CLOSE
WALL STREET’S BLACK FRIDAY
The S&P 500 falls 3.7% to 6,910.42 on Friday and the Nasdaq drops 5.28% — both now in correction, 10% below their highs. Nvidia sinks 13.8% in the worst week since April 2025.
YOUTH  ·  PROTESTS
GEN Z TAKES TO THE STREETS — AGAIN
Friday’s international youth day of action spans 25 countries and some 400 cities — against the cost of living, tuition fees and military spending. Protests roll into Saturday: a transport strike in Italy, rallies in France and Spain.
U.S.  ·  COURTS
PALISADES FIRE: GUILTY ON BOTH COUNTS
Jonathan Rinderknecht, 30, is found guilty on two federal arson counts for the January 2025 fire that killed 12. Sentencing is 17 March 2027; he faces up to 40 years.
CARIBBEAN  ·  HAITI
THREE SOLDIERS KILLED IN HAITI
Three Salvadoran soldiers die handling explosives — the first officially reported deaths since the 2,000-troop deployment began last month. One dies in a Dominican hospital; one more is wounded.
